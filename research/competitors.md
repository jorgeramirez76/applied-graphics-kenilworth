Researched June 2026 to position Applied Graphics. Goal: identify where AG can credibly win.

## National franchises (brand benchmarks)
FASTSIGNS, Signarama, Image360, Alphagraphics — polished, templated sites; broad signage menus; strong local-SEO playbooks; price/turnaround messaging. Useful as UX benchmarks, not local threats.

## What the strong sites do well (adopt)
- Clear service taxonomy with dedicated pages + schema.
- Big, real project photography and category filtering.
- Visible certifications (3M, PDAA/Geek Wraps), warranties, and review counts.
- Prominent quote CTA, click-to-call, and service-area pages.
- Fast, mobile-first, modern type and motion.

## What many do poorly (exploit)
- Generic franchise look / stocky imagery → AG can feel more authentic and local.
- Thin trust proof → AG leads with **48 years + BBB A+ + 3M warranty**.
- Buried contact/hours → AG keeps NAP and call button always visible.

## Applied Graphics' defensible position
1. **Longevity:** in business since **1978 (48 yrs)** — older than most regional rivals.
2. **Certified + warranty:** 3M-certified installers and a real 3M MCS warranty ("we fix it, guaranteed") — a concrete promise, not a slogan.
3. **Everything in-house:** design + print + install under one roof; 6,000 sq ft; vehicles to 14′×53′.
4. **Proof:** name-brand showcase work (Disney tour, NBC food-truck feature) + multi-year durability reviews.
5. **Local + reachable:** a real Kenilworth shop you can visit, owner named (Mario Boccongelli).

## Positioning statement
> "The 48-year, 3M-certified Kenilworth shop that designs, prints and installs wraps **with a real warranty** — for one vehicle or a fleet of 500." Lead with **proof and warranty**, not price.
