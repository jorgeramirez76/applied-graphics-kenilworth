## ⚠️ Identity disambiguation (important)
There are multiple unrelated businesses named "Applied Graphics." This project covers **only** the Kenilworth, NJ company:
- **Correct company:** Applied Graphics Inc. — `appliedgrafx.com` — 718 B Jefferson Ave, Kenilworth, NJ 07033 — (908) 620-9600.
- **NOT this company:** `appliedgraphics.com` is a *different* business in **Amesbury, MA** (phone 978-388-5300). Do not mix their facts, images, or socials.
